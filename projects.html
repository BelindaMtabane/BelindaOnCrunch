PROJECT 01 EDUCATIONAL GAME

UNITY · C# · SDG 6

Drop by Drop:
Flow of Hope.

A runner game inspired by water scarcity in rural South African communities. Guide Zuri through hazards, collect resources and help restore the community's water supply.

View GitHub ↗

A CLOSER LOOK

Drop by Drop: Flow of Hope

01 / OVERVIEW

The idea behind the game.

Drop by Drop: Flow of Hope uses gameplay to introduce players to the importance of clean water and sanitation. Players navigate environments, collect useful items, avoid hazards and work towards restoring water infrastructure.

✦
Core objective

Connect an enjoyable game experience to Sustainable Development Goal 6: clean water and sanitation.

02 / GAMEPLAY

Key features.

  • Four-lane movement and jumping controls.
  • Collectible items and hazards to avoid.
  • Water and health values that influence gameplay.
  • Repair objectives linked to water infrastructure.
  • Tutorial prompts and HUD feedback for players.

03 / PROBLEM-SOLVING

Challenges and solutions.

A

Balancing difficulty

Challenge: Keep gameplay engaging without making it too frustrating for younger players.

Approach: Use readable hazards, clear warnings and playtesting to review resource effects.

B

Explaining objectives

Challenge: Help players understand what to collect and what to avoid while moving.

Approach: Use tutorial instructions, visible HUD values and distinct prompts.

C

Connecting the theme to play

Challenge: Make water scarcity part of the gameplay rather than just the story.

Approach: Link water collection, resource pressure and repair objectives to the main mission.

04 / REFLECTION

Process and learning.

The project combines movement, collectibles, hazards, resource tracking, interface feedback and repair objectives. Building and testing systems in smaller parts helps identify issues before they affect the whole level.

What I learned

  • Connecting mechanics to an educational theme.
  • Making HUD feedback clear during fast gameplay.
  • Breaking gameplay systems into smaller testable parts.
  • Considering age-appropriate difficulty and usability.

The Grove Meadows.

A fantasy tower-defence game where players use defenders, resources and wave-based combat to protect a magical grove from corrupted creatures.

View GitHub ↗

A CLOSER LOOK

The Grove Meadows

01 / OVERVIEW

The idea behind the game.

The Grove Meadows is a fantasy tower-defence project centred on protecting a grove. Players manage resources, place defenders and respond to enemies that move along a path towards a defended objective.

✦
Core objective

Combine resource decisions, defender placement, enemy movement, combat and wave progression into a clear gameplay loop.

02 / GAMEPLAY

Key features.

  • Build locations for strategic defender placement.
  • A resource wallet that informs build decisions.
  • Enemy variants with different combat attributes.
  • Wave-based encounters that increase strategic pressure.
  • HUD feedback for resources and match state.

03 / PROBLEM-SOLVING

Challenges and solutions.

A

Reliable enemy movement

Challenge: Move enemies through the level without relying on a navigation mesh.

Approach: Use waypoint-following and test path order, turning and arrival behaviour.

B

Target selection and combat

Challenge: Keep enemies focused on valid targets and moving when combat ends.

Approach: Define targeting rules for defenders and the tower, then test damage, death and target-loss cases.

C

Balancing enemy types

Challenge: Make enemy types feel different without making one unfair.

Approach: Tune health, speed, damage and rewards together, then test combinations across waves.

04 / REFLECTION

Process and learning.

This project combines C++ gameplay systems with Unreal Engine workflows. A useful development cycle is to build one system, test it in isolation, connect it to the game loop and verify its behaviour in play.

What I learned

  • Dividing gameplay logic into focused systems and components.
  • Connecting waypoint movement with explicit targeting rules.
  • Balancing enemy attributes as a connected set.
  • Combining code-driven systems with Unreal visual workflows.

Tiny Detective – Word Mystery

01 / OVERVIEW

Every mystery starts with a clue.

Tiny Detective is a browser-based detective puzzle game. Players investigate cases by examining evidence, solving scrambled-word challenges and identifying suspicious characters.

The game combines word reconstruction with detective decision-making. Players must pay attention to the available information before making an accusation.

🔍
Core objective

Solve the mysteries by interpreting clues, completing word puzzles and identifying the correct culprit.

02 / GAMEPLAY

Key features.

  • Multiple detective cases and mystery scenarios.
  • Scrambled-word reconstruction puzzles.
  • Suspect investigation and evidence collection.
  • Clue-based decision-making and culprit selection.
  • Points and credibility tracking.
  • Purchasable hints to assist with difficult puzzles.
  • Interactive game feedback and case progression.

03 / PROBLEM-SOLVING

Challenges and solutions.

A

Word puzzle logic

Challenge: Create scrambled-word challenges that remain understandable and solvable.

Approach: Use JavaScript to manage word data, shuffle characters, validate answers and provide feedback.

B

Investigation decisions

Challenge: Make players think about evidence instead of simply guessing the culprit.

Approach: Connect clues, suspects and accusations through the game's investigation logic.

C

Game feedback and progression

Challenge: Keep players informed about their progress and the consequences of their decisions.

Approach: Track points, credibility and hints using JavaScript game state.

04 / REFLECTION

Process and learning.

Tiny Detective combines interface design, word manipulation, game-state management and investigation mechanics. Its development involves connecting individual puzzle systems into a larger detective experience.

What I learned

  • Creating interactive word-based game mechanics.
  • Managing game progression using JavaScript.
  • Designing clue-based puzzle experiences.
  • Connecting player decisions to game outcomes.
  • Using CSS to establish a recognisable visual identity.

PROJECT 05 MEMORY / STRATEGY GAME

HTML · CSS · JAVASCRIPT · GAME AI

Memory Mayhem:
The Royal Debt.

A humorous royal memory battle where players challenge King Reginald in a quest to escape a ridiculous 10,000-gold-coin debt. Match cards, remember symbols, use special abilities and outsmart the king.

A CLOSER LOOK

Memory Mayhem – The Royal Debt

01 / OVERVIEW

A royal debt needs a clever solution.

Memory Mayhem – The Royal Debt is a turn-based browser memory game where players compete against King Reginald.

The game uses a 16-card board containing eight matching pairs. Players flip cards, remember symbol positions and attempt to collect more pairs than the king.

The experience combines traditional memory gameplay with special abilities and an opponent that uses memory to make its decisions.

👑
Core objective

Defeat King Reginald in the memory challenge and escape the 10,000-gold-coin debt.

02 / GAMEPLAY

Key features.

  • 16-card board with eight matching pairs.
  • Turn-based player versus King Reginald gameplay.
  • Card flipping and matching mechanics.
  • Opponent memory and card-position tracking.
  • Score, turn and matching-pair tracking.
  • Streak-based rewards and progression.
  • Special abilities that change the matching challenge.

Royal abilities

  • Lucky Hint: Helps reveal useful card information.
  • Switcheroo: Changes the matching challenge.
  • Royal Shuffle: Rearranges the cards.
  • Chaos Card: Introduces an unpredictable twist.
  • Royal Shield: Provides defensive protection.

03 / PROBLEM-SOLVING

Challenges and solutions.

A

Opponent memory

Challenge: Create an opponent that can remember previously revealed cards without having perfect knowledge.

Approach: Track revealed card positions and use the stored information when making the king's moves.

B

Ability management

Challenge: Introduce special abilities without removing the importance of the core matching mechanic.

Approach: Manage ability availability, activation, cooldown or reward conditions through the game's state.

C

Turn-based game logic

Challenge: Ensure that card selection, matching and turn transitions happen in the correct order.

Approach: Use controlled game states to manage card selection, match results and opponent turns.

04 / REFLECTION

Process and learning.

Memory Mayhem combines classic matching mechanics with opponent decision-making and ability systems. The project demonstrates how a familiar game concept can be extended through progression, strategy and character-driven interactions.

What I learned

  • Building turn-based gameplay systems.
  • Tracking card states and matching pairs.
  • Implementing opponent memory and decision logic.
  • Managing special abilities and unlock conditions.
  • Balancing strategy with accessible gameplay.
