A LITTLE ABOUT ME

I turn ideas into interactive, memorable experiences.

MY STORY

Curiosity inspires the way I create.

My journey into development has given me a way to combine imagination with practical skills. I enjoy taking an idea that may initially exist only as a concept and working through the steps needed to turn it into something people can experience.

Game and application development allow me to explore different ways of thinking. I can approach a challenge as a designer, consider its technical requirements as a developer and think about how the final product will feel to the person using it.

I am naturally curious and willing to learn. When I encounter a difficult problem, I try to break it down, investigate what is happening, experiment with possible solutions and learn from the outcome.

I believe growth comes from being open to feedback, asking questions and being willing to improve. Every project gives me a new opportunity to strengthen my skills and discover a better way of doing things.

MY CREATIVE PROCESS

From an idea to an experience.

I enjoy the full journey of development: understanding a challenge, imagining possibilities, building systems and refining the details that bring everything together.

01

Imagine

I explore ideas, identify the purpose of a project and think about the experience I want to create. I consider the audience, the problem and what could make the solution engaging.

02

Design

I turn ideas into a clearer plan. This includes thinking about layouts, interactions, gameplay, level structure and how different elements should work together.

03

Build

I develop the features and systems that make the idea work. I enjoy creating gameplay mechanics, interactive interfaces, levels and practical technical solutions.

04

Integrate

I bring different parts of a project together, considering how the code, interface, assets and interactions connect to create one coherent experience.

05

Refine

I test, troubleshoot and improve. Small details matter, and I want the final experience to feel clear, considered and enjoyable rather than simply finished.

06

Keep learning

I use challenges and feedback as opportunities to grow. I am always looking for ways to improve my technical understanding, creative decisions and approach to problem-solving.

PURPOSE & INNOVATION

Creating with people in mind.

I am interested in how games and applications can make ideas easier to understand, encourage learning and respond to real-world challenges. I believe technology becomes more meaningful when it serves a clear purpose and considers the people who will use it.

This influences how I approach my projects. I think about more than whether a feature works. I also consider whether it makes sense, whether it helps the user and whether the overall experience is engaging and memorable.

TEAMWORK & LEADERSHIP

Good ideas grow through collaboration.

I enjoy working on projects where people bring different ideas and skills to a shared goal. Collaboration has helped me understand the importance of communication, planning and considering how individual contributions fit into a larger vision.

Through my roles as team lead and project manager on Fiscal Flow and Drop by Drop: Flow of Hope, I have gained experience in coordinating work, supporting project progress and helping keep development focused on the intended goals.

I value a practical approach to leadership: understanding what needs to be done, communicating clearly, working through challenges and helping the team move forward.

I also recognise that teamwork involves listening, adapting and learning from other people. I want to continue growing as both a developer and a collaborator who contributes to a positive, productive creative environment.
