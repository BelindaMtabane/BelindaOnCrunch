BEHIND THE CODE & THE CREATIVITY

Hi, I'm Belinda. I turn ideas into interactive experiences.

I'm a Game Design and Development student at IIE Vega College, interested in building interactive worlds, experimenting with gameplay systems and finding creative ways to bring ideas to life.

My journey brings together programming, visual design, problem-solving and storytelling. I enjoy exploring how these elements can work together to create experiences that are engaging, thoughtful and meaningful.

HOW I THINK

Creative thinking, practical problem-solving.

I see game development as a combination of imagination and technical problem-solving. A good idea needs more than an exciting concept; it also needs systems, structure, testing and thoughtful design.

I'm interested in experiences that do more than entertain. For example, my water-conservation game explores how gameplay can introduce players to an important real-world issue.

I'm still growing as a developer, and that is part of my process. I value curiosity, learning from mistakes, collaborating with others and steadily improving my work.

WHAT I'M BUILDING

Ideas in progress.

UNITY · C#

Drop by Drop: Flow of Hope

An educational runner inspired by UN Sustainable Development Goal 6: clean water and sanitation. The game explores water collection, environmental hazards and resource management.

My focus: runner systems, spawning, pickups, hazards and foundational UI and tutorial work.

Development and refinement ongoing.

ANDROID · KOTLIN

Fiscal Flow

A financial application concept focused on making budgeting and money management easier to navigate through a clear, engaging interface.

My focus: team leadership and contributions to the dashboard, monthly budget, transactions, profile and gamified screen UI.

Firebase integration was planned and is not represented as completed.

UNREAL ENGINE · C++

The Grove Meadows

A fantasy forest tower-defense project centred on defenders, enemy behaviour and protecting a magical environment from approaching threats.

My focus: enemy variants, defender types, interface elements and aspects of spawning and behaviour.

Procedural-generation work is in an early stage.
