MY JOURNEY

Learning by building.

Every project is an opportunity to learn something new, solve a problem and turn an idea into something people can explore.

01

Designing the experience

I enjoy thinking about how players interact with a game, how its systems fit together and how its design can communicate an idea clearly. I consider the experience from the user's perspective, not just the technical side.

02

Building the systems

I turn ideas into working features by developing mechanics, creating levels, programming behaviour and connecting different parts of a project. I enjoy seeing individual pieces become a complete interactive experience.

03

Improving through iteration

Testing, debugging and refinement are important parts of my process. When something does not work as expected, I investigate the problem, explore alternatives and use what I learn to improve.

HOW I THINK

Creative thinking. Practical problem-solving.

I see development as a combination of imagination and technical problem-solving. A good idea needs more than an exciting concept; it also needs structure, thoughtful design, working systems and testing.

I'm especially interested in experiences that do more than entertain. For example, Drop by Drop: Flow of Hope explores water scarcity and clean water awareness through interactive gameplay.

I am still growing as a developer, and that is an important part of my journey. I value curiosity, learning from mistakes, collaborating with others and steadily improving the work I create.

FROM CONCEPT TO CREATION

How I bring ideas to life.

My process combines creative exploration with practical development, from the first idea to the details that make an experience feel complete.

01

Imagine & plan

I explore the purpose of a project, think about its audience and plan how the experience should work before building its main features.

02

Develop & integrate

I work on the mechanics, interfaces, levels and systems, bringing different components together so they support the same vision.

03

Test & refine

I test features, troubleshoot issues and refine the experience. I aim to make the result clearer, more reliable and more enjoyable to use.

TEAMWORK & LEADERSHIP

Building together, moving forward.

I have taken on team lead and project manager responsibilities on Fiscal Flow and Drop by Drop: Flow of Hope. These experiences have helped me understand the importance of planning, communication and keeping a team focused on shared goals.

I enjoy considering how each person's contribution fits into the bigger picture. I aim to approach challenges constructively, coordinate tasks and help bring different parts of a project together.

For me, good collaboration means listening, adapting, sharing ideas and being willing to learn from others while working towards a common outcome.
